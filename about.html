ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Arjin Talway

Arjin Talway is the author of “Sprache und Kultur entdecken”, a German learning project for levels A1–A2.

The project combines learning the German language with cultural insights into Germany and Kurdistan.

Sprache und Kultur entdecken

The project includes a book and a companion learning website with audio texts, dialogues, vocabulary, grammar and exercises.
